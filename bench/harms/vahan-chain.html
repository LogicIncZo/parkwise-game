One plate from a name

The spec logs a plate per session and never mentions VAHAN — it doesn't have to. In India, a plate is a foreign key to a national register of owner-linked vehicle records.

The harm

The spec never says the word VAHAN. It doesn't need to. In India, a plate is a foreign key to the national vehicle register: VAHAN holds registration records for on the order of 28–40 crore vehicles, and a plate number is enough to pull up registration details — make, model, insurance, fitness, RC status, and owner-related fields.[3][4]

Public-facing lookups have been hardened over time — the open "know your vehicle" service now returns a partially masked owner name to anonymous users.[3] That is a real improvement, and it does not dissolve the chain. The same plate resolves through SMS-based lookup services,[4] through a large ecosystem of third-party "RC check" apps and reseller APIs operating in a grey zone, and through enforcement channels with full access. A parking ledger that logs a plate per session, per place, per timestamp is therefore one join away from identities — the platform may never run that join itself, and may still enable it by retaining the key.

The harm isn't hypothetical in kind, only in degree: plate-linked data is already the raw material of India's e-challan phishing industry, where fraudsters impersonate traffic authorities with plausible vehicle details to extract payments.[5] Every additional database that maps plates to people and places enlarges the training set for exactly these schemes.

The claim to test: does the ULB's parking concession grant the vendor (or its partners) API access to VAHAN or RC data? If yes, the platform's privacy boundary is not the API spec — it is the contract the public hasn't read.

Ask your city

  • Does the parking vendor or any partner hold API credentials for VAHAN / RC lookups, and under what authority?
  • Are plate-to-owner resolutions from the parking ledger logged, auditable, and disclosed in privacy notices?
  • What is the retention period for vehicle_plate after a session closes — and is it ever hashed or truncated for analytics?
  • Which third parties (BAP apps, collections agencies, analytics firms) receive plate-level records?
